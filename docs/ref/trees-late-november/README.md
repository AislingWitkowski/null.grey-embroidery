# Trees, late November in Ohio (Aisling's photos, Nov 25, 2026)
Reference only, for building Embroidery's trees. **These are not places in the game** (Aisling, Nov 25). Her own photos; anything identifying (signs, car plates) is not to be copied into the world.

1. `1-branch-gold-leaves`: long dark branches, half bare, the last gold, rust and red leaves in clusters near the tips.
2. `2-canopy-gold-leaves`: a big tree from below: thick dark limbs, gold leaves still dense on the inner branches, thinning outward.
3. `3-lot-lamp-bare-trees`: young planted trees, fully bare; a row of rust and gold trees behind; grass still green with scattered brown leaves.
4. `4-sycamore-last-leaves`: a young sycamore still holding big olive-gold leaves while the trees around it are bare.
5. `5-bare-crown`: a fully bare crown: one trunk, many fine upright twigs, a pale grey-brown fan against the sky.
6. `6-young-bare-tree`: a thin young tree with long whip-like upper twigs; a mulch ring at its foot.
7. `7-hawthorn-red-leaves`: a thorny, twiggy tree with a few bright red leaves left on one side only.

**For building:** in late November most trees are bare or nearly bare; the leaves that stay cluster near branch tips or on one side; colours are gold, rust and a few reds, never even; fine twig fans read against the sky; grass stays green with leaves scattered on it. Embroidery's skies are mostly overcast (doc 33), so take the shapes and colours from these, not the blue sky.
